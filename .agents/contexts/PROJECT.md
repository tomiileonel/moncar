# Project Context — MonCar

## Product
- Name: MonCar
- Purpose: Vehicle fleet management, tracking, and operational maintenance platform.
- Primary users: Fleet managers, drivers, dispatchers, and operations directors.

## Critical flows
- Fleet vehicle status tracking and assignment.
- Operational logs, fuel logs, and scheduled maintenance alerts.
- Admin authentication and role-based management.
